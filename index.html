A deeper view of how you naturally work, decide and respond.

Aurora Spectrum looks at six behavioural Spectrums, Applied Judgement, Workplace Reasoning and the type of work that tends to energise you. Personality items deliberately present two reasonable preferences — choose what is genuinely more like you, not what sounds more impressive.

How to answer: choose the option that best reflects your natural tendency. There are no ideal personality results. For the paired questions you must lean toward one of two reasonable preferences; there is intentionally no neutral middle choice.
